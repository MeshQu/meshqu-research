## OCDS duplicate-fetch defect

**Surfaced post-run by direct inspection of `decision_traces.jsonl`. Not in the live-notes — Sam should treat this as a new observation.**

`decision_traces.jsonl` has 300 lines but only **283 unique OCIDs** and **283 unique decision_ids**. Decomposed:

| Pattern | OCIDs | Extra trace lines |
|---|---|---|
| OCID appears 7× | 1 | +6 |
| OCID appears 2× | 11 | +11 |
| Total dupe OCIDs | **12** | **+17** |

The 7× repeat OCID landed at record_indices **68, 143, 191, 240, 252, 253, 265** — spread across most of the run, not adjacent. The 11 pair-dupes are spread similarly: 66+71, 77+95, 100+105, 148+166, 171+175, 182+193, 210+211, 212+214, 225+228, 244+246, 268+269.

**Diagnosis** — the duplicate trace pairs share:
- Same `decision_id`
- Same `receipt_timestamp` (e.g. `0cf6e7ee-…` at indices 66 + 71: `receipt_timestamp = 2026-05-18T10:45:29.746Z` on both)
- Same MeshQu verdict
- Different `ts` (local write timestamp) — the second post happened ~30s after the first

That shape means **only one MeshQu evaluation actually occurred per OCID**. The runner POSTed the duplicate, MeshQu's idempotency returned the cached receipt (identical `receipt_timestamp`), and the local trace recorded both POSTs. MeshQu state is therefore uncorrupted — there are 283 receipts on the server side, not 300.

**Root cause is upstream of the runner**, in the OCDS substrate-fetch path. The runner faithfully evaluated whatever the fetch returned. Two plausible hypotheses (not investigated this session per the run-day discipline):

1. **OCDS adapter pagination bug** — overlapping page windows could re-yield records near page boundaries. The pair-dupes are mostly adjacent or near-adjacent in record_index, consistent with this. But the 7× repeat doesn't fit pagination.
2. **OCDS feed re-publication** — UK Contracts Finder publishes amendments and updates against the same OCID. The substrate adapter doesn't deduplicate by OCID before yielding. The 7× repeat is consistent with a single contract with multiple amendment notices in the publication window.

**Practical implications for analysis:**
- All cross-record analysis must dedupe by `decision_id` (or `ocid`) before computing rates. The verdict / agreement / rule-firing / substrate sections above are deduped. Raw counts are also recorded for the audit trail.
- The corpus has effective n = **283**, not 300. Pre-registration P1 / P2 bands stated against 300; the gap is small enough not to invalidate them, but the writeup must report n = 283 honestly.
- The 7× repeat OCID is itself a research artefact worth investigating — a single procurement record producing 7 evaluations across the corpus is a stability/idempotency demonstration even if it wasn't intentional.

Follow-up: post-run investigation session to (a) identify which OCID is the 7× repeat and inspect its raw OCDS records, (b) decide whether to add OCID-deduplication to the substrate adapter, (c) decide whether to re-fetch + re-run for a clean 300-unique corpus or accept n=283 as the headline.
